GOOD TO KNOW — 06

A few useful answers.

Will the cabin size fit every airline?

No single dimension fits every airline. The listed 55 × 35 × 23 cm is a product-demo size; check the airline’s current cabin baggage allowance before departure.

Can I change my design later?

Yes. Change the shade, finish, hardware, size and monogram in the configurator before adding the current design to the mock cart.

Are these prices and policies live?

No. Prices, stock, delivery estimates, warranty and returns are illustrative portfolio-demo data and are not a real offer to sell.

How should I care for the shell?

For a typical hard-shell suitcase, wipe with a soft damp cloth and mild soap, then dry fully. Avoid abrasive cleaners and test any product on a small area first.
